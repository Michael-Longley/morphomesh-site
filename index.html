Available Now

Four tools, ready today.

These tools are already functional in the app, with more on the way.

Model Painter

Paint a model face-by-face, band-by-band, or region-by-region — solid color, height bands, normal-based fill, flood fill, brush, or project a photo straight onto the mesh. Export a multi-color file your slicer already understands, ready for multi-material printing.

Color Puzzle Generator

Turn any photo into a multi-color puzzle — color regions are detected automatically and split into pieces ready for multi-material printing.

Kit Card Generator

Selling kits or miniatures? Drop in your part files and Morphomesh lays them out on one printable sheet, connected with support sprues — like a store-bought model kit.

3MF Merger

Combine multiple 3MF files into one without losing color or material assignments.
