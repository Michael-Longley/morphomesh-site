The pitch

Morphomesh is a pre-slicer design suite. It gets your model ready for printing before it ever hits the slicer. Today we're showing two tools: the Color Puzzle Generator and the Model Painter.

Who it's for: novice modelers who want to customize existing models and make them their own, and experienced modelers who want to take their pieces from 3D prints to products.

The two tools

Color Puzzle Generator

Turn an image into a model, as a color-separated puzzle or a print-in-place piece.

Input
An image: vector, PNG, JPG and more. Simpler images work best.
Output
A 3MF with each color as its own model.
Modes
Print in Place: one solid piece, zero gap.
Puzzle: separate pieces with adjustable tolerance.
How it works
  1. Load your image
  2. Tune resolution and color count using the preview
  3. Merge, skip, or set colors as background
  4. Configure the model: mode, tolerance, thickness
  5. Generate
Then slice and print as usual.
Show them
#1 Album Coasters for Print in Place. Dragonfly, Love, Punk Monkey or Tulip for Puzzle.
Be upfront about
16-color cap for now (likely lifted before beta). Busy images and photos get simplified.

Model Painter

Customize your favorite model with images, QR codes, text, or hand-painted designs.

Input
A 3D model (STL, 3MF), plus an optional image.
Output
3MF object(s) with the colors painted on.
Paint methods
Image projection, hand painting, text, QR codes.
How it works
  1. Load your STL or 3MF
  2. Pick a paint method
  3. Configure it and move it into place
  4. Apply it; repeat steps 2–4 as needed
  5. Optionally edit existing layers
  6. Export the 3MF
Then slice and print as usual.
Show them
#10 Flag Duck for mirrored sides. #14 Double Decker Bus for layered images.
Be upfront about
Undercuts (surfaces that curl back on themselves) don't work. Some shapes warp the image. More colors means more purge.

Questions

General

Why not just do this in my slicer?

Slicers are built to slice. Bolting model-editing features onto them makes them more complex and more likely to break. A separate prep step lets us add any feature we want, work across every slicer, and not wait on their release cycles.

Will it work with my printer, AMS or MMU?

Yes, the suite works with any printer. For Model Painter specifically, AMS and MMU setups produce a lot of purge waste compared to tool changers, so tool changers are the best fit for painting.

Is it free? What will it cost?

It won't be free. Pricing is still being worked out, and we're looking seriously at a one-time purchase option.

Bring up the hardware survey (see Wrap-up).

Can I use it on files I bought or downloaded?

Yes. Any geometry file works: bought, self-modeled, or downloaded from a model repository. Printing or selling the results still follows that model's own license.

Selling what you make? We're planning a commercial license for the suite; details are still being decided.

Point them to the mailing list.

When can I get it?

No release date yet. We're working toward a limited closed beta. The best way to hear first is the mailing list.

Does it run locally or in the cloud? Do I need a powerful PC?

Hybrid, to save users money. Run it locally on stronger hardware, or eventually queue jobs on our cloud platform.

Perfect lead-in to the hardware survey.

How long does processing take?

Depends on the model, roughly 1 to 10 minutes.

Can I tweak the result in my slicer?

Absolutely. We're not replacing your slicer. We handle the complex operations up front, and you fine-tune before printing.

Can I use both tools on the same model?

Yes. There are a few steps in between right now, and we're working to smooth that out as we add more tools.

Is the Mando Helmet / Zelda Sword official?

No. They're fan pieces, not officially licensed. We made them to show off the tools, using public domain or CC parts and AI-generated images.

Color Puzzle Generator

Is there a color limit?

16 for now. That's a printer limit more than a software one, and it'll likely be removed before the beta.

Can I pick or merge colors?

Colors are auto-detected from the image composition. You can merge them in the tool.

What about gradients, shadows, or photos?

They get simplified: merged into neighboring colors, or given an in-between color for the gradient section.

Is it separate pieces or one print?

Your choice, with two modes. Print in Place has zero gap between colors, so it prints as one piece that can't come apart. Puzzle makes separate pieces with a tolerance you set.

These may become two separate tools later.

What's the minimum detail size?

Configurable, based on your nozzle. It also cleans up noise that won't print well. Like lithophanes, Print in Place pieces can be printed vertically for better resolution.

Can I set thickness, backing, or a border?

Yes. You choose which color is the background (or none) and the thickness of the parts and backing.

Do the pieces need glue?

Print in Place comes out as one piece, no glue. Puzzle pieces fit as tight or loose as you set them, depending on your printer's tolerance.

Can I use my own logo or artwork?

Yes, with no limits beyond what your printer can do. Clean lines and distinct colors work best.

Selling them? Mention the planned commercial license.

Model Painter

How do I place the image?

You position, rotate, and scale it yourself. You can also ignore colors to remove backgrounds and change resolution for more detail.

How fine can the detail get?

It depends on print orientation. Printing flat, you're limited by nozzle size. Printing tall, you're limited by layer height.

Is it surface only, or deeper?

You set the paint depth. Deeper wraps better but can cause odd colors in spots. It also enables clean mirrored sides: the same image, flipped, on both faces.

Show #10 Flag Duck.

How many colors can one model use?

As many as your printer can handle.

Does it work on complex shapes?

It works on concave and convex shapes. Some shapes warp the image, and undercuts (surfaces that curl back on themselves) don't work.

Can I paint by hand?

Yes. You can paint by hand, or add text and QR codes. The image is optional.

How much filament goes to purge?

It depends entirely on the model. You're adding more colors than a normal print, so expect more purge. Tool changers help the most.

Will it work in my slicer?

It ships ready for Bambu Studio, PrusaSlicer, OrcaSlicer, and Snapmaker Orca. For any other slicer, you paint a provided sample file once and it learns how to paint for that slicer.
